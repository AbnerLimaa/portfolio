## Welcome to my first blog post!

It's a pleasure to have you here. I've been planning to start this project for a while now to share some ideas that occasionally cross my mind. This is a project I intend to carry on for a lifetime, but I won't commit to a publishing frequency 😄 (Maybe I'll post once a week).

Some creators served as inspiration for me to take the decision to start a blog, and I would like to mention them here as they produce excellent content worth checking out:

1. Teiva Harsanyi from https://read.thecoder.cafe/p/about
2. Alex Rios from https://alexrios.me/about/
3. Rafael Miguel from https://chroniclesofapragmaticprogrammer.substack.com/about

## What is this blog about?

Here you will find me talking about topics related to **Computing**, such as: **frameworks**, **libraries**, **languages**, **software engineering**, **systems architecture**, **project management**, **cloud**, **AI**, and much more. Furthermore, I intend to give my two cents on the technology market in Brazil and around the world. So stay tuned and see you later!
